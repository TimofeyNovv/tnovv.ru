## Exact prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: cinematic landscape background for a minimalist personal website, wide 16:9.
Primary request: a secluded mountain lake and dense conifer forest at blue hour, almost nighttime, enveloped in abundant drifting fog. Natural, realistic, serene, moody, beautiful. No abstract graphics.
Composition: dark pine trees on both shores frame the left and right edges, several overlapping forested mountain ridges recede into the center distance. Lake in lower third, a soft hazy horizon across the middle. The center upper half has very dark low-detail cloudy blue-black sky and soft fog, suitable for white text laid over it. Trees and forest must remain visible and recognizable across lower half, not just at the very edges, so a centered mobile crop also feels natural. Thick luminous silver blue banks of low mist roll between the trees and over the water, creating depth. Fine ripples and faint forest reflections near the bottom.
Lighting/mood: nocturnal diffused ambient light, charcoal black, slate blue, muted gray green, silvery fog. Gentle tonal contrast. A convincing atmospheric photograph, polished, subtle film grain, organic rather than overprocessed.
Constraints: no people, no buildings, no lights, no moon disc, no circles, no cherry blossom trees, no flowers, no text, no logo, no watermark. No bright or white sky. Keep the center dark enough for white website typography. Lots of natural low-lying mist, never smoke.
